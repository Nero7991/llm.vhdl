# 2026-09-20: where the 660,600 cycles of a B job go

## The question

> THE MEASUREMENT (MEASURED on the FK33 card 2026-09-20,
> hw/fk33/results/card_swg_2026-09-20/profile/profile_flat_tok0.txt and
> profile_striped_tok0.txt, per-step cycle attribution via the seam's
> STEPS_ISS/ISSUE_CYC counters at 75 MHz core clock): every B_JOB step costs
> 660,600 cycles, constant to within 30 cycles across all 24 layers and
> identical on the flat and the lane-striped HBM image. The 24 B jobs are
> 15.85 M of the token's 61.9 M cycles on the flat image and about half of
> the 30.1 M-cycle token on the striped image, so B is now the largest
> single cost. DERIVED from the RTL geometry (hw/fk33/gen_fk33_card.py
> WDOG_LIMIT comment): one recurrent pass is 32*(128/4)*128 = 131,072
> cycles, and the tiered store moves 1,101,824 B in and 1,101,824 B out over
> a 256-bit port = 68,864 beats each way, plus the per-layer gdn_const image
> of 66,048 B (fourth load-only phase, docs/2026-09-18_b-constants-path.md).
> If load, compute and store are fully serial and the AXI port streamed one
> beat per cycle, that is about 131k + 138k + 2k = 271k cycles; the card
> measures 660k, so either the mover runs at roughly 4 to 5 cycles per beat,
> or phases are longer than derived, or something else is in the job.
>
> THE JOB: account for the 660,600 cycles in simulation and find the
> cheapest change that removes the most of them.

Date 2026-09-20. Build: the card bitstream of 2026-09-20 (`B_STATE_AXI`,
`B_SRC_REAL`, `B_CONST_HBM`, `B_RECUR_LANES 4`, `B_CONV_LANES 4`, the
store's default `MAXOUT 4` / `MAXB 16`, 75 MHz). Symptom: `B_JOB` steps of
660,601 / 660,622 / 660,616 cycles (profile_flat_tok0.txt steps 7, 23, 39).

## The answer

**644,172 of the 660,600 cycles are accounted for in simulation (2.5%
short), and the bound is the mover's per-beat handshake, not HBM.**
`gdn_state_axi` as shipped accepts ONE AXI beat, drops RREADY, unpacks its
WPB words into the store at one word per cycle, then raises RREADY again:
WPB+1 cycles per beat, 5 for the mantissas. Its save requests WPB words,
waits the two-edge read, presents the beat and waits for WREADY before
requesting the next: WPB+3, 7 for the mantissas. The 32,768 mantissa beats
alone cost 163,887 + 229,393 = 393,280 cycles, 61% of the job. HBM latency
swept 0 / 40 / 80 cycles moves the total by 4x the latency, i.e. it is paid
once per load phase and is otherwise invisible; the R channel is
back-pressured for 192,700 cycles (data waiting while the mover unpacks)
and the W channel is idle for 228,654 of the save's 263,086 cycles.

**The fix, implemented behind two generics defaulting to the old
behaviour:** `WIDE` (a beat-wide port on `gdn_state_mem`, the mantissa mover
moves one beat per cycle) and `PIPE` (the other three movers overlap the
handshake with the unpack). Same bench, same 770,965 value checks green:
**644,172 -> 307,784 cycles per job (-52%)**, which is 24 x 336k = 8.07 M
cycles per token, 108 ms at 75 MHz. `gdn_block`'s own run, MEASURED at the
9B generics, is 149,579 of the remainder and is untouched.

Everything below is MEASURED with GHDL unless labelled otherwise.

## The procedure

1. **Read the RTL for the phase structure.** `rtl/gdn_job_seq.vhd` runs
   one layer as: store LOAD -> `gdn_block` -> tap refill (2,048 groups) ->
   store SAVE, strictly serial. `rtl/gdn_state_store.vhd` runs a load as
   four sequential movers over one master pair (mantissas 1,048,576 B,
   exponents 4,096 B, conv taps 49,152 B, constants 66,048 B) and a save as
   the first three. Every mover is `rtl/gdn_state_axi.vhd` at a different
   `WORD_BITS`: 64 (WPB = 4 words per 256-bit beat), 8 (WPB 32), 16 (WPB
   16), 16. `MAXB = 16` (AXI3 cap), `MAXOUT = 4` bursts, no read FIFO: one
   beat register.

2. **Derive the per-beat cost from the FSM.** Load: accept (1) + WPB
   unpack cycles with RREADY low = WPB+1. Save: WPB requests + 2-edge
   latency + 1 present + 1 accept = WPB+3, with the read port idle while W
   is presented. DERIVED at 9B: mantissa 32,768 x (5 + 7) = 393,216; exp
   128 x (33 + 35); conv 1,536 x (17 + 19); const 2,064 x 17; total mover
   ~492k, plus recurrence, plus refill.

3. **Measure `gdn_block` alone at the 9B generics.** `sim/tb_gdn_block.vhd`
   with `KEY_HEADS=16 VAL_HEADS=32 DIM=128 RECUR_LANES=4 CONV_LANES=4
   SILU_LANES=8 NLAYER=1 TOKENS=1`, producers eager. This is the quantity
   the mover bench cannot produce and the one the derivation had at
   131,072.

4. **Build `sim/tb_bmover_phases.vhd`**: `gdn_job_seq` + `gdn_state_store`
   at the 9B geometry wired as `llama_top`'s `gen_st_tier`, an AXI3 slave
   with PIPELINED latency (every burst's data ready `RD_LAT` cycles after
   its AR, independent of the bursts ahead) at one beat per cycle, and a
   stand-in `gdn_block` that holds `busy` for exactly the measured run
   while reading back and checking every word the load delivered, then
   writing a new pattern the save must carry. Phases attributed by the
   address region of the most recent AR/AW (GHDL 1.0.0 mcode cannot
   elaborate an external name on the store's `sel`; the boundary error is
   1-2 cycles per phase). One `BMOVER_PHASE <name> <cycles>` line each.

5. **Sweep latency 0 / 40 / 80** on the shipping mover, then implement the
   two levers and sweep again, plus `MAXOUT` 4 / 8 / 16.

6. **Teeth**: mutants of the new paths against the bench, with the
   attribution control (each mutant also run in the modes where its code
   is dead), then a second, adversarial pass of the bench with random and
   long stalls on every channel and AR accepts synchronised to RLAST.

Every mutant dies in every mode where its code is live, and every control
passes. "bound check" is GHDL's range check on `outst`/`pend`/`wcred` going
out of range, i.e. the counter defect caught before it becomes a wrong
number; in hardware the same counter wraps silently.

**M8 is the shipping mover.** It dies only under the adversarial pass
(AR accepts held to RLAST cycles, 17 coincidences in shipping mode); the
random-stall slaves of `tb_gdn_state_store` and `tb_gdn_state_axi` never
reach the coincidence, which is why the hazard survived every gate since
2026-09-02. The gate's default row runs PIPE+WIDE, where the shipping load
path is dead, so M8 is guarded by this document's explicit
`-gPIPE=false -gWIDE=false` run and not by a gate row.

**Resolution floor, MEASURED, and the reason the bench has a second
pass:** with a clean slave, M3, M5 and M7 survived `tb_bmover_phases` in
every mode while `tb_gdn_state_store`'s stalling slave killed all three.
With random per-cycle stalls added, M3 (WPB=16 needs 16+ consecutive W
stalls), M7 (needs AW throttled, i.e. B withheld past MAXOUT bursts) and
M9 (needs an AR accept on the RLAST cycle, ~(1/4)^14 by chance) still
survived. Each got its own axis: 48-cycle W and AW stalls every ~1,500
cycles, 96-cycle B stalls every ~1,100, and `arready` synchronised to the
beat the slave will present next. **A random stall is not a long stall,
and neither is a coincidence.**

## Measured and REJECTED -- do not retry

- **"HBM latency with too few outstanding reads."** Latency 0 / 40 / 80 on
  the shipping mover: 644,012 / 644,172 / 644,332. Raising `MAXOUT` on the
  shipping mover saves nothing (it already hides 320 cycles). Do not spend
  a track on outstanding depth until the consumer is one beat per cycle.
- **"HBM bandwidth / the striped image."** The card's own measurement says
  flat and striped are identical for B; the bench's slave is full-rate and
  the R channel is back-pressured 192,700 cycles. The port is waiting on
  the mover, not the reverse.
- **"The recurrence is longer than derived."** It is, 149,579 against
  131,072, but that is 18.5k of the 389k gap and it is the block's conv and
  norm passes, all previously documented.
- **"A per-beat bubble on the card's read path explains the residual."**
  The 09-18 bitstream (no `B_CONST_HBM`) measured 617,226; the constants
  phase is 35,132 in the bench, so the two bitstreams differ by 43,375, i.e.
  21 cycles per constants beat against 17 in the bench. If that 4-cycle
  bubble applied to every load beat it would add 146k, more than the whole
  residual, so it does not; the 09-18 build also lacked `B_SRC_REAL`, which
  changes B's input path. Two bitstreams, several variables, no
  attribution. Not retried; the residual is listed as open.

## Measurement traps hit

- **GHDL 1.0.0 mcode dies on an external name** (`translate_name: cannot
  handle IIR_KIND_EXTERNAL_SIGNAL_NAME`, a GHDL internal error, not a
  VHDL one). The phase attribution had to come from the AXI address
  instead; the boundary error is stated above.
- **A signal array of 8.8 M `std_logic` costs ~2 GB in GHDL** (228 B per
  scalar signal, the recorded `stmem` finding). The slave's memory is a
  shared variable; the bench peaks at 3.4 GB, almost all of it the store's
  own 131,072 x 64-bit `mem` signal.
- **`natural` is 31 bits here.** `constant SBASE : natural := 2**32`
  analyses with a bounds warning and elaborates to garbage; the bench's
  arenas sit at 2**30.
- **The mutant runner's "NO VERDICT" was the harness**: the first table
  ran without `ulimit -s unlimited` and every 9B run died silently on
  stack before printing anything. A control row (a mutant in a mode where
  its code is dead) returning NO VERDICT rather than PASS was the tell.
- **A 64-bit LCG in a per-cycle slave costs 85 s of GHDL per pass**;
  xorshift32 costs nothing. 94 s -> 15 s per default run.
- **A random 1/3 W stall never holds W for 16 cycles**, so a WPB=16
  assembly overrun is unreachable by it. Long stalls are a separate axis.
- **The blanket rename `wv_q -> wv_r` inside the process also renamed the
  WIDE branch's accept detection**, which must read the concurrent
  `wv_q`; the WIDE save then re-sent one beat forever and the slave's
  WLAST assert caught it on the first run.
- **A latent hazard in the shipping load path, found while writing the
  fast paths**: an AR accept and an RLAST accept on the same edge wrote
  `outst <= outst + 1` then `outst <= outst - 1`, and the later assignment
  wins, so the count ran one low. Consequence: one burst more than
  `MAXOUT` in flight and `S_LDRAIN` able to report done with a burst still
  returning, whose beats the NEXT phase's mover would accept as its own
  data. Reachable in the shipping mover only when an AR stall ends on the
  exact RLAST cycle; the fold is now applied in all three paths and the
  bench's stalled pass forces the coincidence (17 times in the shipping
  mode). Whether it has ever fired on the card is not known.

## Open, not yet answered

- **The 16,429-cycle residual (2.5%)** between the bench's 644,172 and the
  card's 660,601. Candidates: `gdn_block`'s producers on the card being
  slower than the bench's eager ones; small per-beat bubbles in the HBM
  path (register slices, the clock converter); the seam's step accounting
  including issue overhead. None measured.
- **The HBM read latency at 75 MHz.** Estimated 40-80; decides whether
  `MAXOUT` 4 is enough once `WIDE` is on (it is at 64 or below).
- **Synthesis of the banked `gdn_state_mem`.** The `WIDE` form is four
  banks of 32,768 x 64 with a shared read address and a 4:1 output mux
  after the register. Whether Vivado infers 32 URAM288 for it as it does
  for the flat array has NOT been checked (no Vivado in this track); take
  the census before quoting it, and read the mapping report, not the log.
- **The three small movers' per-beat cost** (exp 32, conv 16, const 16
  cycles per beat even with PIPE) is now 90,540 of the 307,784: the next
  lever is a beat-wide port on `gdn_conv_tap_mem`, `gdn_conv_w_mem` and
  `gdn_exp_mem`, files this track does not own.
- **`tb_gdn_block`'s 149,579 is with eager producers**; the card's run is
  not measured separately.

## 2026-09-20, later: synthesis of the banked store (TRACK BMOVERSYN)

The "Synthesis of the banked `gdn_state_mem`" item above is closed. Four
OOC draws of `gdn_state_store` at the 9B `u_state` generics on the BC-250
(Vivado 2023.2, one tool at a time, `MemoryHigh=8G`, none at cap), by
`sim/ooc_bmover_run.sh` / `sim/ooc_bmover.tcl`; raw numbers, per-instance
attribution, census files and worst paths in
`hw/fk33/results/bmover_ooc_2026-09-20/README.md`.

- **The banked memory IS 32 URAM288**, four banks of eight, named
  `u_mem/gwide.gb[k].bank_reg_uram_0..7` in the census and listed as
  `RAM_SDP 32768x64` per bank in `report_ram_utilization`. `[Synth 8-10226]`
  and `[Synth 8-7186]` appear zero times in all four logs; `[Synth 8-5780]`
  names each bank with cascade height 8. Log, census and RAM report agree.
- **Cost of both levers: +1,004 LUT / +1,040 FF** (DERIVED from the table),
  nothing in BRAM, URAM or DSP. WIDE alone is +185 LUT and -308 FF (the
  256-bit `wbuf` becomes a 19 x `RAM32M16` FIFO); PIPE's cost is entirely in
  the three narrow movers (`u_edma`, `u_cdma`, `u_kdma`, each about +250 to
  +295 LUT and +280 to +535 FF), and `u_dma` is identical with or without
  PIPE once WIDE is on. `MAXOUT 8` over 4 is +1 FF; its -109 LUT is scatter
  in `u_cdma`, not a saving.
- **Timing is not worse; the estimate is 0.36 ns better.** In every draw the
  worst path is the URAM read cascade (7 `CAS_IN -> CAS_OUT` hops, about
  2.8 ns) into the mantissa mover; the control adds two `LUT5` levels of
  narrow-word select into `wbuf` (9 levels, 3.527 ns), the WIDE variants
  land the URAM output straight on the FIFO's LUTRAM (7 levels, 3.062 ns).
- Against the placed 75 MHz card (363,095 of 439,680 LUT, 32 URAM), the
  delta is +0.2 points of LUT; the parts do not sum across contexts, so it
  is an order of magnitude and not a card figure.

**Verdict: safe to enable.** `PIPE => true, WIDE => true, MAXOUT => 8` in
`rtl/llama_top.vhd`'s `u_state` map, as the ranked-fixes table asks. Still
open after this: routed timing on the card, and the B-job cycle count on
silicon.

Census trap recorded in the README: `REF_NAME =~ DSP*` under `-hier` counts
each DSP48E2 nine times (macro plus eight leaf cells); use
