TRACK GAIN16 -- SECOND PREDICTION, registered 2026-08-31 BEFORE the `swcb`
CSV existed (verified: SWCB_STILL_RUNNING at the moment of writing).

WHY A SECOND ONE.  My first prediction was fitted to a mode-grid argument and
was falsified by two of its own falsifiers.  The replacement mechanism -- 9
RAMB36 per bit of stored word -- was DERIVED FROM three of the six points it
explains, so it has not yet predicted anything it was not fitted to.  A model
that reproduces its own calibration points is worth nothing; that is the exact
failure CLAUDE.md records, where a one-parameter model fitted to one point was
12 percentage points wrong on the next.  This is the out-of-sample test.

THE MECHANISM, and its KNOWN EXCEPTION stated up front:

  MEASURED   9-bit -> 81   11-bit -> 99   14-bit -> 126     all exactly 9.0000/bit
  MEASURED  16-bit -> 135, where the rule predicts 16 x 9 = 144.

  So the rule holds at 9, 11 and 14 and FAILS at 16 by exactly one bit plane.
  It is NOT a law and must not be quoted as one.  See RECONCILIATION below.

PREDICTED for `swcb`, the codebook (266,240 x 11 index ROM + 1,567 x 16
codebook requested `distributed`):

  RAMB36    99      The index store is the SAME SHAPE as the sw11 probe
                    (266,240 x 11), which MEASURED 99.  The codebook is
                    asked for distributed and should take none.
  RAMB18    12      unchanged, rmsnorm_rs_mem's three vector banks
  tile     105      = 99 + 12/2
  DSP       41      MUST NOT MOVE.  This is the gating criterion, not a
                    prediction: ROUTE2 measured the routed design DSP-bound
                    at 80.63%, so any DSP movement makes the lever a
                    regression whatever it does to BRAM.
  CLB LUT  5,700 .. 6,300   ESTIMATE.  sw11 measured 5,061 and a 2,048-deep
                    16-bit distributed ROM is roughly 16 x 32 = 512 LUT6 plus
                    mux, so 600-1,200 added.  ASSUMPTION: Vivado honours
                    `rom_style = "distributed"` on a combinationally-read
                    constant array.
  WNS      +0.7 .. +0.971   ESTIMATE.  The codebook inserts one LUT level
                    between the index ROM's registered output and the unit's
                    bank write port, on a path that had 0.971 ns of slack.

GAP, DERIVED, against TRACK ROUTE2's MEASURED composed 253.5 of 372.5:
  253.5 + 99 = 352.5  ->  +20.0 TILES SPARE, i.e. the 16-tile gap CLOSES.

FALSIFIERS, named now so the answer only has to be classified:

  G1  RAMB36 above 105.  Then the codebook took block RAM despite the
      attribute, or the index store is not the shape sw11 measured, and the
      99 must not be quoted.
  G2  DSP not 41.  Then the lever is a REGRESSION and must not land, however
      many tiles it wins.
  G3  CLB LUT above 8,000.  Then the combinational codebook costs more than a
      registered BRAM stage would, and the pipeline-stage version is worth
      the NN+3 load it would cost.
  G4  WNS negative.  Then the added LUT level does not fit and the codebook
      must be registered.
  G5  RAMB36 exactly 144 or exactly 135.  Either would mean the index ROM did
      not narrow at all and the whole mechanism is wrong.

WHAT THIS PREDICTION CANNOT SETTLE: the elaboration time.  `swcb` had already
been in Vivado's RTL elaboration for over 20 minutes when this was written,
against 425 s for the whole of `head`, so whatever the area says, the
generated form's elaboration cost is a separate and possibly disqualifying
number.  `swcb2` is the control for that and is queued behind it.
