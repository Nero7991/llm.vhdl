Vivado maps this ROM as one 32Kx1 cascade PER BIT PLANE -- ceil(266240/32768)
= 9 -- so cost is LINEAR IN WORD WIDTH and indifferent to how bits are grouped.
That is why splitting 14 bits into 9+4+1 across three arrays costs 126, the
same as one 14-bit array, to the tile.  DO NOT RETRY SPLITTING.
