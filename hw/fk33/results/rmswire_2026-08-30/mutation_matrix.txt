=== the w_active tap, judged by sim/tb_rmswire_loadrace.vhd ===
The tap feeds an assertion that never fires in a correct design, so
tb_llama_top_normw cannot see it break.  The loadrace bench TIMES both
gain-reading passes off it, so a broken tap moves every boundary it
derives.  That is the only check with teeth on this pin.
