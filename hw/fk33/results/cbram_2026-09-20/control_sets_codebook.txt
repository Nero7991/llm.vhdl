## reading
  build 10 : 1,536 x 16 bels = 24,576 LUTRAM bels.  16 bels is one RAM32M16
             (14 RAMD32 + 2 RAMS32).  One private write port per copy.
  build 11b:    48 x 128 bels = 6,144 flip-flops.  128 = 16 entries x 8 bits,
             i.e. the whole codebook in registers, ONE BANK PER RANK.
  The 1,536 read ports did NOT merge: MUXF8 rose by 1,536 x 8 = 12,288.

  This is also the first tool COUNT of CBFANOUT's central claim on a real
  build: distinct command-register nets 1,536 -> 48.
