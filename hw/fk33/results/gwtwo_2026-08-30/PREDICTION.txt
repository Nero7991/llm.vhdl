THE SHAPE, DERIVED
------------------
  NN      = SHAPE.hidden = 4096          (rtl/model_cfg_pkg.vhd, MODEL 9B)

  bits    = 65 * 4096 * 16 = 4,259,840

  A RAMB36 holds 32,768 data bits + 4,096 parity bits.
  4,259,840 / 32,768 = 130.0 EXACTLY.  So 130 tiles is the data-only floor at
  ANY aspect ratio, and 116 is the floor if the parity bits are usable.

  MEASURED at GW = 4 (66,560 x 64): 171 RAMB36.  That is 76.0% of the
  data-only capacity of 171 tiles, i.e. Vivado is ~1.32x off the floor.

PREDICTION 1 -- THE MAIN ONE
----------------------------
  BRAM tile count is approximately GW-INVARIANT: every point in
  {GW=1, 2, 4, 8} lands within +/- 20% of 171.

  WHY.  The total bit count is fixed by the model, not by GW.  A RAMB36E2 on
  this device natively supports 32Kx1, 16Kx2, 8Kx4, 4Kx9, 2Kx18, 1Kx36 and
  512x72 (SDP).  The four candidate word widths are 16, 32, 64 and 128 bits,
  and EVERY one of them sits on a native width with the same 8/9 padding loss
  (16 of 18, 32 of 36, 64 of 72, 128 as 2x72).  The depth divides evenly in
  every case (NW_N*NWORD = 266240 / 133120 / 66560 / 33280, all exact
  multiples of the corresponding native depth).  There is no pathological
  aspect ratio in this set, so there is no reason for the tile count to move.

  CONSEQUENCE IF IT HOLDS: GW = 2 does NOT close the 51-tile gap and does not
  meaningfully narrow it.  The lever NORMURAM named is not a lever.

PREDICTION 2 -- LUT
-------------------
  LUT moves by less than 500 between the extreme points, and monotonically
  with GW: the only GW-dependent logic in the block is the GW-to-1 sub-word
  mux on 16 bits (zero LUT at GW=1, ~16 LUT6 at GW=4) and the ROM's own
  depth-cascade output mux, which moves the OTHER way (deeper at small GW).
  So the two effects partly cancel and the curve may not even be monotone.

  CONSEQUENCE: if prediction 1 holds, THERE IS NO EXCHANGE RATE TO STATE.
  Tiles-saved-per-LUT-added is 0/x = 0 at every point, which is not a trade,
  it is a null result.

PREDICTION 3 -- LOAD-RATE MARGIN
--------------------------------
  The margin does not move at ANY GW, and the brief's "at GW = 2 the margin
  halves to 2.0x" is stale.  See CORRECTION_TO_BRIEF.txt: TRACK RMSWIRE
  already replaced the GW-wide load with a one-element-per-cycle stream, so
  the load is NN + 2 cycles INDEPENDENT OF GW.

P1  BRAM is approximately GW-invariant (+/- 20%, falsified by > 34 tiles)
    ** FALSIFIED, by its own criterion. **
    MEASURED 135 / 145 / 171 / 226 RAMB36 at GW = 1 / 2 / 4 / 8.
    GW = 1 is 36 tiles below GW = 4, and 36 > 34.
    GW = 8 is 55 tiles ABOVE, so the full span is 91 tiles on a 171 base.
    The reasoning was sound clause by clause and assumed Vivado packs to the
    primitive's capability.  It does not: efficiency against the RAMB36's
    32,768 data bits is 96.3% / 89.6% / 76.0% / 57.5%, degrading monotonically
    as the word widens.

P2  LUT moves by less than 500 between the extremes, possibly non-monotone
    ** HELD, and it was monotone. **
    5,073 / 5,229 / 5,265 / 5,277.  Span 204 < 500.  Monotone increasing in GW,
    so the sub-word mux dominates and the depth-cascade effect I expected to
    push the other way did not appear at this resolution.
