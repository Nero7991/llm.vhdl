THE TWO TERMS
  FIXED cost per call: ~7.5 us, FLAT from 64 B to 1024 B, still only 8.37 us
  at 4 KB.  The fixed term dominates everything below about 8 KB.
  PER-BYTE: peaks near 1.5 GB/s at 64 KB, then FALLS to ~0.94 GB/s at 4 MB.

  That fall is NOT explained.  Do not model this interface as a single
  throughput figure, and do not extrapolate the large sizes.

WHY IT WAS TAKEN
  A whole token (docs/debugging/2026-08-30_a-whole-token-on-the-silicon.md)
  cost 3,583,291 MMIO reads at 1.92 us = 6.885 s of a 17.814 s token, because
  Y_IDX/Y_LO/Y_HI is the only result path.  Whether an HBM result path helps
  turns on the FIXED cost, since one 96-row job's Y is under 1 KB -- a path
  that is fast per byte and expensive per call would lose at that size.

  It does not lose.  768 B sits deep inside the flat region at ~7.5 us.

  This measures the HOST side of an EXISTING path.  It says nothing about
  whether the engine can write Y to HBM, what that costs in LUT against a fit
  that is not settled, or what it does to a WNS of +0.069 ns.  TRACK READBACK
  owns those questions and this file is an input to it, not an answer.
